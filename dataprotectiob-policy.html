Data Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2, 2025

This Data Privacy Policy outlines how The Unlimited Youths (TUY) collects, processes, and protects personal data in compliance with applicable data protection laws and regulations. It applies to all personal data collected through our website, social media platforms, events, surveys, fundraising activities, and other interactions.

1. Introduction

The Unlimited Youths (TUY) is committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of personal data collected, used, and maintained during its operations. This policy establishes the principles and guidelines for the collection, processing, and protection of personal data.

2. Scope

This policy applies to all personal data collected, processed, and stored by TUY, including data obtained through our website (https://www.theunlimitedyouths.org), social media platforms, events, surveys, fundraising activities, and other interactions with individuals and organizations.

3. Data Collection

TUY collects personal data for purposes such as providing services and programs, conducting research, fundraising, communication, volunteer engagement, and human resources management. The types of personal data collected may include:

  • Names
  • Contact information (e.g., addresses, phone numbers, email addresses)
  • Demographic information (e.g., age, gender, occupation)
  • Employment history and qualifications

4. Data Processing

TUY processes personal data lawfully, fairly, and transparently, only for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes. Processing activities may include:

  • Storage and retention
  • Access and retrieval
  • Sharing and disclosure
  • Analysis and reporting
  • Implementation of security measures

5. Data Security

TUY employs appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data during transmission and storage
  • Access controls and authentication mechanisms
  • Regular security assessments and audits
  • Staff training on data protection best practices

6. Data Retention

TUY retains personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable laws. Data is kept for a maximum of five years, after which it is securely deleted, anonymized, or archived in accordance with internal data retention policies.

7. Data Sharing

TUY may share personal data with third-party service providers, partners, or other organizations only when necessary and with appropriate safeguards. Data sharing may occur:

  • With consent from the individual
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect the vital interests of individuals
  • For the performance of a contract or agreement

8. Individual Rights

Individuals have the following rights regarding their personal data:

  • The right to access their personal data
  • The right to rectify inaccurate or incomplete data
  • The right to restrict or object to the processing of their data
  • The right to request the deletion of their data
  • The right to data portability

Requests to exercise these rights should be submitted in writing to our Data Protection Officer (DPO) at info@theunlimitedyouths.org. TUY will respond in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

9. Complaints

If you believe your personal data has been processed in violation of applicable data protection laws, you may lodge a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

10. Updates to the Policy

This Data Privacy Policy is reviewed annually and may be updated to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on our website and, where appropriate, communicated to affected individuals.

Contact Us

For questions or concerns about this Data Privacy Policy, please contact our Data Protection Officer at info@theunlimitedyouths.org.
