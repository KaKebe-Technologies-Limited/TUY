Cookie Policy

Last Updated: March 21, 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how Unlimited Youth uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience on our website, https://www.theunlimitedyouths.org.

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device to enhance your experience on our website. They help us remember your preferences, improve site functionality, and provide personalized content.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential: Enable core functionality (e.g., form submissions).
  • Analytics: Track site usage (e.g., Google Analytics) to improve content.
  • Marketing: Personalize offers (e.g., newsletter signups).

How We Use Cookies

To remember your preferences, analyze traffic, and deliver relevant updates. Cookies help us understand how you interact with our site to provide a better user experience.

Managing Cookies

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may limit site functionality. Check your browser’s help section for instructions on managing cookies.

Third-Party Cookies

We use Google Analytics and payment processors, which may set their own cookies. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for more information.
