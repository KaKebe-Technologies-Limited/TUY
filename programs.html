Poverty Reduction Program

Empowering Uganda's youth with practical skills and financial knowledge to break the cycle of poverty

Breaking the Cycle of Poverty

Our Poverty Reduction Program equips young Ugandans with essential life skills, financial literacy, and mentorship to build sustainable livelihoods and create economic opportunities.

Youth Life Skills Training

We provide comprehensive vocational training in market-relevant skills including tailoring, mechanics, agro-economy, and emerging trades. Our hands-on approach ensures youth gain practical experience.

  • 6-month intensive training programs
  • Industry expert instructors
  • Practical workshops and field experience
  • Career counseling and job placement

Financial Literacy

Financial education is critical for economic independence. Our workshops cover essential topics to help youth manage money effectively and build sustainable livelihoods.

  • Budgeting and savings strategies
  • Investment principles for beginners
  • Microfinance and small business funding
  • Debt management and avoidance

Mentorship Programs

Our mentorship initiative connects youth with successful professionals who provide guidance, support, and industry insights to help navigate career paths.

  • One-on-one mentorship pairings
  • Group mentorship sessions
  • Career development workshops
  • Networking opportunities

ICT Training

Digital skills are essential in today's economy. Our ICT training provides youth with fundamental computer skills and technology knowledge.

  • Basic computer literacy
  • Software applications training
  • Internet and digital communication
  • Digital entrepreneurship skills

Innovation Program

Fostering creativity and technological advancement among Uganda's youth through research and tech innovation

Driving Innovation Through Technology

Our Innovation Program empowers youth to become problem-solvers through research, technology camps, and hackathons that address real-world challenges in Uganda.

Field Research Projects

Our research initiatives tackle pressing challenges facing Uganda's communities. Youth researchers work alongside academic mentors to conduct field studies and develop innovative solutions.

Research Focus Areas:

  • Agricultural technology and food security
  • Renewable energy solutions
  • Healthcare accessibility in rural areas
  • Educational technology innovations
Research Workshops

We organize 4 intensive workshops annually to train youth in research methodologies, data analysis, and solution design.

Field Research

Participants conduct hands-on research in communities to understand real challenges and co-create solutions.

Publishing

We support youth in presenting their findings at conferences and publishing in academic journals.

Tech Camps & Hackathons

Our tech camps bring together young innovators to develop technology solutions for community challenges. Through intensive hackathons, participants build prototypes and pitch their ideas to potential investors.

Annual Tech Events:

  • Uganda Youth Tech Challenge (March)
  • East Africa Innovation Summit (July)
  • Climate Solutions Hackathon (October)
  • Digital Agriculture Challenge (December)
Stakeholder Engagement

We connect with government, industry, and academic partners to identify challenges and resources.

Funding Acquisition

Our team secures funding from sponsors and partners to support tech events and winning projects.

Event Execution

We organize and host impactful tech camps that bring together youth from across Uganda.

Climate Action Program

Mobilizing youth to combat climate change through advocacy, tree planting, and green job creation

Taking Action for Our Planet

Our Climate Action Program engages youth in environmental conservation, climate advocacy, and sustainable livelihood creation to build a greener future for Uganda.

Climate Advocacy & Awareness

We empower youth to become climate champions through education, media engagement, and community mobilization.

Radio Talk Shows

We conduct 4 radio talk shows annually on local stations to discuss climate issues and solutions, reaching rural communities across Uganda.

Social Media Campaigns

Our digital campaigns reach thousands with engaging content about climate action, sustainability tips, and environmental education.

Climate Seminars

We organize 4 climate action seminars each year in schools and communities to build awareness and inspire local action.

Collaborative Partnerships

We partner with environmental organizations to amplify our impact and share resources for climate action.

Tree Planting Initiative

Our ambitious reforestation program aims to plant 10,000 trees annually and create sustainable green jobs in Uganda.

10,000 Trees Campaign

We mobilize youth volunteers to plant 10,000 native trees each year in deforested areas and community spaces.

Raising Nursery Beds

We establish and maintain nursery beds to raise diverse species of fruit and timber trees for our planting initiatives.

Distribution of Seedlings

We distribute seedlings to communities and schools to promote widespread tree planting and environmental stewardship.

Partner with Climate Change Organizations

We collaborate with climate-focused organizations to enhance our reforestation efforts and share best practices.

Green Jobs Promotion

We create sustainable employment opportunities in forestry, agroforestry, eco-tourism, briquette production, craft making using plastic waste, and solar installation training.
