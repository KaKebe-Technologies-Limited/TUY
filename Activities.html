Our Programs

Transforming Uganda through strategic programs aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goals

Strategic Programs

Through evidence-based interventions and community partnerships, we're building a sustainable future for Uganda's youth and communities.

Poverty Reduction

Empowering Uganda's youth with practical skills and financial knowledge to break the cycle of poverty and build sustainable livelihoods.

  • Youth Life Skills Training

    Comprehensive vocational training programs in tailoring, mechanics, agro-economy, and emerging trades.

  • Financial Literacy Workshops

    Essential financial education covering savings strategies, investment principles, and budgeting techniques.

  • Mentorship Programs

    One-on-one and group mentorship focusing on leadership development and entrepreneurship guidance.

Innovation & Technology

Bridging the digital divide by equipping Uganda's youth with cutting-edge technology skills and fostering an innovation mindset.

  • ICT Training Programs

    Comprehensive digital literacy programs covering essential computer skills and emerging technology applications.

  • Tech Camps & Hackathons

    Interactive platforms where youth collaborate to design innovative, tech-driven solutions to real-world problems.

  • Research Initiatives

    Evidence-based research projects that tackle modern challenges facing Uganda's communities.

Climate Action

Leading the fight against climate change through direct environmental action, community engagement, and sustainable livelihood creation.

  • Tree Planting Initiatives

    Ambitious reforestation program targeting 10,000+ trees planted annually in partnership with local communities.

  • Climate Advocacy Campaigns

    Multi-channel awareness campaigns including radio shows, social media drives, and community seminars.

  • Green Jobs Promotion

    Strategic collaborations to create eco-friendly employment opportunities that make sustainability profitable.

Our Skills

At TUY, we focus on equipping the youth with a wide range of skills, from creativity and leadership to entrepreneurship and community development. Our programs are designed to foster growth in various key areas that empower the youth for the future.

Empowering Through Skill Development

At TUY, we believe in hands-on learning, enabling the youth to develop real-world skills in a variety of fields. From creative arts to leadership and community service, we foster talent that creates a lasting impact in society.

  • Leadership Development
  • Creative Arts & Design
  • Entrepreneurship & Innovation
  • Community Engagement
  • Environmental Awareness
  • Health & Wellness Advocacy

About Us

The Unlimited Youths (TUY) empowers Ugandan youth through awareness, capacity building, and mentorship, targeting employment and leadership by 2029, with programs in poverty reduction, climate action, and innovation.
