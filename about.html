About The Unlimited Youth

    About The Unlimited Youths

    The Unlimited Youths (TUY) is an organization with a vision of fostering prosperous and successful youth in Uganda. TUY achieves this by focusing on awareness creation, capacity building, and mentorship. By 2029, TUY aims to enable 1000 youths to secure gainful employment, become leaders of change, and thrive across Uganda. The organization's strategic approach includes strengthening its institutional capacity and ensuring sustainability, while implementing programs centered on Poverty Reduction, Climate Change/Action, and Innovation.

    Our Mission

    To empower the youth to solve 21st-century developmental challenges through awareness creation, capacity building, and mentorship.

    Our Vision

    Prosperous and successful youth in Uganda.

    What we do?

    TUY tackles 21st-century challenges through youth-focused programs in poverty reduction, climate action, and innovation.

    What We Offer

    TUY is a youth-driven organization striving towards solving 21st-century developmental challenges via awareness, capacity building, and mentorship, with a strategic focus on Institutional Strengthening and Sustainability. Key ongoing programs include Poverty Reduction, Climate Change/Action, and Innovation, supported by initiatives such as life skills and financial literacy training, mentorship, ICT education, research, tech camps/hackathons, climate change advocacy and awareness, tree planting, and the promotion of green jobs.
